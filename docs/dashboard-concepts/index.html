Five directions for the home page

Each one uses the same real content (NorthPointe's systems, Wednesday Practice checklists, wiki pages), with the clock fixed at Saturday 10 October, 6:40 pm and one access point offline, so you see how a problem surfaces. Open a page for the full, interactive version. Each look ships in both a light and a dark scheme, and also works at phone width.

Dark scheme

1. Toward Sunday

The page is organised around the week. A Monday to Sunday rail shows what happened, where "now" is, and Sunday as the destination, with an hourly health strip under each day. Below it, a "Ready for Sunday" check turns the systems, the team, the stream and the help desk into one score.

Signature
The week rail with a moving "now" marker
Feel
Light, crisp, practical. Archivo, navy and marigold
Best for
Everyone. It answers "are we ready?" at a glance
Cost
Medium. Needs a small "week events" API that merges incidents, tickets and plans

3. Glass

A stained-glass window, after Richter's Cologne window, where every pane is one hour of one system: lit when it was up, dark when it was down. Each kind of system has its own glass colour. The content below sits in a lead frame as light panes. Hover a pane to see what it is.

Signature
The live uptime mosaic
Feel
Bright, jewel-toned, modern. Albert Sans
Best for
Wow factor that is also a real 12-hour uptime chart
Cost
Medium. Services and hosts already keep history (monitor checks, infra rollups). Wi-Fi devices and switches are live-only today, so they would need a small hourly up/down table

4. Front of House

Built for the tech team. Each system area is a channel strip with a scribble strip, a live meter and a status LED. The main strip carries the recent alerts and a mute button that is maintenance mode. The next service is on the "scene" screen with a show clock and run sheet. The team is an input list, and your checklist can be ticked off in place.

Signature
The channel strips
Feel
Hardware, dense, fun for techs. Barlow Condensed
Best for
The booth screen and the people who run Sunday
Cost
Medium. The meters want one live figure per area (CPU, latency, clients and so on)

5. Start page

Search first and plain language. One sentence sums up the state of things, with the important bits as links. The page is one prioritised "Needs you" list with actions you can take in place (acknowledge, take a ticket, with undo), and a narrow side column for the service, systems and recent pages.

Signature
The written summary and in-place actions
Feel
Airy, friendly, the calmest option. Figtree, current brand periwinkle
Best for
Volunteers and staff who come to find something or do one thing
Cost
Low to medium. Needs a "needs you" feed across modules
